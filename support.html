I Need Support

If you're sleeping rough, in temporary accommodation, at risk of losing your home, or struggling right now — you don't have to work this out alone.

🛟 If You're in Immediate Danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please call 999.

If you're struggling emotionally or need to talk to someone right now, Samaritans are free to call, any time, day or night: 116 123.

If you're worried about domestic abuse, the National Domestic Abuse Helpline is free and confidential: 0808 2000 247.

🤝 What This Actually Looks Like

We do not simply hand people a list of organisations. We help you understand your options, make contact, complete the necessary steps, and keep moving forward — including:

  • Contacting the correct council homelessness team with you
  • Completing housing and homelessness applications
  • Gathering identification and supporting evidence
  • Helping you prepare for or attend appointments
  • Explaining letters and decisions in plain language
  • Connecting you with shelters and supported accommodation
  • Challenging it if you're being passed between services
  • Keeping track of your referrals and following up

That's supported navigation and advocacy — not just a signpost.

What We Don't Do

RemoteAbility does not provide emergency accommodation or clinical rehabilitation directly. We help people access the right services, complete referrals and applications, overcome barriers, and remain supported throughout their journey towards housing, recovery, work and independence.

After You Reach Out

What Happens Next?

1

We Get in Touch

A member of our team contacts you using the method you chose, to understand your situation properly.

2

We Work Out What You Need

Together we identify the most urgent priorities — whether that's housing, benefits, health, or something else.

3

We Help You Take the Next Step

We support you to contact the right services, complete applications, and prepare for appointments — and we stay involved.

4

We Keep Going With You

As things stabilise, we support you towards recovery, confidence, skills and — when you're ready — work or self-employment.

Getting in Touch on Behalf of Someone Else?

If you're a professional, family member, or friend reaching out for someone else, please use our referral form instead.
