Before You Apply

Is RemoteAbility Right for You?

✅ You're Likely a Good Fit If:

  • ✓You've experienced homelessness or are in recovery, and are now stable and ready to build skills
  • ✓You're disabled, neurodivergent, or have a mental health or long-term health condition
  • ✓You're currently unemployed or underemployed
  • ✓You want to work remotely from home
  • ✓You're motivated to learn new digital skills
  • ✓You can commit to at least 5-10 hours per week initially
  • ✓You have access to the internet (mobile or broadband)
  • ✓You're based in the UK

Not there yet? That's fine — I Need Support can help you reach this stage first.

🔍 What We're Looking For:

  • No prior experience required—we start with a full assessment and train from scratch.
  • Willingness to learn: You'll be working with new tools, systems, and clients.
  • Reliable communication: Respond to messages, attend check-ins, ask for help when needed.
  • Commitment to your pathway: Stay engaged with training and client work.
  • Openness about adjustments: Tell us what you need to succeed—we'll work with you.

We don't require qualifications, CVs, or interviews. Just honesty about your situation and commitment to the process.

After You Apply

What Happens Next?

1

We Review Your Application

Our team reads every application carefully. We're looking for commitment and fit—not qualifications.

2

Intro Call (No Interview!)

If you're a good fit, we'll invite you to a casual 20-minute call to answer questions and discuss next steps. No pressure, no judgment.

3

Onboarding & Setup

You'll be assigned a pathway coordinator, receive your welcome pack, and sort out any equipment or support you need to get started.

4

Training Begins

Join your cohort, meet your team, and start learning. Within 3 weeks, you'll be working on real client projects.
