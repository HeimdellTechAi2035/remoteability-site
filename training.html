Six-Week Pathway to Independence Programme

Six weeks of practical support towards stability, skills, employment, self-employment and sustainable independence.

📚 One Programme, Not Three Separate Trainings

The RemoteAbility Pathway to Independence Programme supports homeless, disabled, neurodivergent and disadvantaged people to overcome barriers, access essential support, and move towards sustainable work, self-employment or business ownership. This is the detailed, practical curriculum within the Ready and Independent stages of our wider Pathway.

The programme recognises that not everybody begins from the same position. Some participants may be ready to start a business immediately. Others may first need support with housing, addiction recovery, mental or physical health, benefits, identification documents, banking, digital access, confidence, daily routines, or employment preparation. That's why the programme opens with its own assessment and stabilisation stage — not everyone needs it, but everyone gets the chance to use it.

Programme Structure

Three Two-Week Stages

1

Safe and Stable

Weeks 1–2
2

Weeks 3–4
3

Work and Independence

Following the most suitable pathway into employment, freelancing, self-employment or business ownership.

Weeks 5–6
Choosing a Route

Four Possible Progression Routes

By the end of Week 2, every participant chooses one primary pathway — full details are covered in Stage One.

A. Employment

Part-time, full-time, remote, flexible or supported work, or a gradual return to work.

B. Freelancing / Self-Employment

Selling a skill or service independently — virtual assistance, content, cleaning, gardening, repairs, admin, design, photography, tutoring, delivery and more.

C. Starting a Business

For participants with a viable idea, reasonable stability, and the capacity to find and serve customers.

D. Preparation and Recovery

Recovery, volunteering, routine-building and confidence work — still progress, not failure.

How It's Delivered

Programme Delivery Model

👥

Group Workshops

Two group workshops per week covering skills, digital tools, employment, business, sales, AI, confidence and practical exercises.

🤝

One-to-One Mentoring

Every participant receives at least one individual session each week, reviewing personal barriers, referrals, progress, goals, safeguarding, adjustments and next actions.

⚡

Practical Action Sessions

Supported time to make calls, complete forms, apply for work, contact customers, prepare documents, attend online appointments, use digital tools and follow up referrals.

What Success Looks Like

Participant Pathways at Completion

A participant may complete the programme with one of several valid outcomes. Nobody is marked unsuccessful simply because they haven't entered work within six weeks — for somebody beginning in crisis, obtaining identification, engaging with rehabilitation, or securing accommodation may be the most important outcome.

1. Employment Ready

A CV, job-search strategy, completed applications, interview preparation, reasonable-adjustment support, and an application tracker.

2. Freelance Ready

A defined service, pricing, a customer profile, a professional profile, a prospect list, and completed outreach.

3. Business Launch Ready

A viable business concept, a clear offer, pricing, a basic business plan, a business profile, and customer outreach underway.

4. Stability and Preparation

Active housing or recovery referrals, a support plan, improved digital access, a stable routine, a volunteering or training pathway, and continued mentoring.

Common Questions

Programme FAQs

Do I need any previous experience to join?

No. The programme is designed for complete beginners and starts with a full needs assessment — not an assumption that you're already work-ready.

Do I have to choose employment, freelancing or business straight away?

No. You choose your progression route at the end of Week 2, once you've had a full assessment and a chance to discover your strengths. If you're not ready for any of those yet, Pathway D (Preparation and Recovery) is a valid and supported route.

Is the programme fully remote?

Yes. All training, mentoring and practical action sessions are delivered remotely. You can take part from anywhere in the UK with an internet connection — and we can help with digital access if you don't have it yet.

What if I only need help with housing or recovery, not training?

That's completely fine — visit I Need Support or see the Safe and Stable stage of Our Pathway. This six-week programme is for people who are ready to move into structured skills-building and work preparation.

Is the programme free?

Yes. All training is provided at no cost to participants. If you need additional equipment or adjustments, let us know and we'll help you find a practical solution.

What happens after the six weeks?

Every participant leaves with a ninety-day progression plan and an ongoing mentoring schedule. Support doesn't stop at Week 6 — we review progress at completion, thirty days, ninety days, six months and twelve months.
