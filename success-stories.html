💼 Pathway A: Employment
Participant • Cohort 2

"I went from benefits to earning £1,800 a month—working from my sofa."

Before RemoteAbility

  • Situation: 3 years unemployed due to chronic pain condition
  • Barriers: Mobility issues, inconsistent energy, commuting impossible
  • Income: Universal Credit and PIP only
  • Confidence: "I thought my working life was over at 34."

The Journey

"I was skeptical at first—how could I do sales when some days I can't even get out of bed? But RemoteAbility's sales training was different. They taught me to work flexibly, use scripts when my brain fog was bad, and manage my energy around calls.

After three weeks of training, I started making real client calls. My first commission cheque was £147—it doesn't sound like much, but I cried. It was the first money I'd earned in years.

By week 6, I was hitting targets regularly. RemoteAbility helped me sort out a laptop, ergonomic chair, and headset so I could work comfortably. Now I work 20-25 hours a week, when my body allows, and I'm earning more than I ever did in my 'healthy' office job."

Where They Are Now

  • Role: Freelance Sales Development Representative
  • Income: £1,600-£2,200/month (commission-based)
  • Hours: 20-25 hours/week, self-paced
  • Next Step: Building client base to reach £2,500+ monthly target
"RemoteAbility didn't just teach me sales—they gave me back my independence, my identity as a worker, and hope for the future."
🧑‍💻 Pathway B: Freelancing
Participant • Cohort 1

"I'd never heard of SEO. Now I manage campaigns for 6 local businesses."

Before RemoteAbility

  • Situation: Long-term mental health challenges, anxiety around office environments
  • Background: No tech experience, left school at 16
  • Income: ESA and housing benefit
  • Confidence: "I thought tech jobs were for other people, not someone like me."

The Journey

"When I joined RemoteAbility, I could barely use Google. The idea of 'optimizing websites' sounded like science fiction. But the trainers broke it down into small, manageable steps—no judgment, just support.

I started with local SEO audits for small businesses. My first client was a plumber in Manchester. I helped him rank #1 for 'emergency plumber Manchester,' and his bookings doubled. Seeing real results from my work was incredible.

The team helped me manage my anxiety through it all—flexible deadlines, regular check-ins, and permission to take mental health days without guilt. Six months in, I'm working with 6 local businesses and earning a proper wage."

Where They Are Now

  • Role: Freelance Local SEO Specialist
  • Income: £1,800-£2,400/month (retainer clients)
  • Hours: 25-30 hours/week, flexible scheduling
  • Next Step: Expanding into content strategy and technical SEO
"I never thought I'd have a career, let alone one where I help businesses grow. RemoteAbility saw potential in me that I didn't see in myself."
🚀 Pathway C: Business
Participant • Cohort 2

"AI tools leveled the playing field. My disability became irrelevant."

Before RemoteAbility

  • Situation: Acquired brain injury, processing speed and memory challenges
  • Barriers: Traditional work environments overwhelming, needed assistive tech
  • Income: PIP and UC, living with parents at 28
  • Confidence: "I used to be sharp. After the injury, I felt useless."

The Journey

"The AI pathway was a revelation. Tools like ChatGPT, Notion AI, and automation platforms became my 'assistive technology'—they filled in the gaps my brain injury created. I could do complex research, write reports, and analyze data with AI support.

RemoteAbility taught me how to structure prompts, chain AI workflows, and use tools like Zapier to automate repetitive tasks. My first paid project was setting up an automated lead capture system for a consultancy. It worked perfectly, and they hired me for more.

RemoteAbility helped me get set up with the right software subscriptions and a second monitor. Now I work on AI implementation projects for small businesses—something I'd never have imagined possible."

Where They Are Now

  • Role: AI Workflow Consultant (contract)
  • Income: £2,200-£2,800/month (project-based)
  • Hours: 15-20 hours/week (high-value work)
  • Next Step: Building SaaS automation packages for niche sectors
"AI didn't just help me work—it gave me back my sense of competence. I'm solving problems again, contributing, earning. I'm myself again."
📈 Progression Story
Participant • Cohort 1 → Team Lead

"I started as a trainee. Now I supervise new participants."

Before RemoteAbility

  • Situation: 5 years out of work due to progressive disability
  • Barriers: Workplace adjustments repeatedly denied in previous roles
  • Income: ESA, struggling financially
  • Confidence: "I'd been rejected so many times, I stopped trying."

The Journey

"I joined the Sales pathway in Cohort 1, not expecting much. But within weeks I was thriving—hitting KPIs, getting great client feedback, earning commissions. For the first time in years, my disability wasn't the story. My performance was.

After 4 months, RemoteAbility asked if I'd be interested in a supervisory role—supporting newer trainees, doing quality assurance, and helping with onboarding. I jumped at it.

Now I'm a salaried Team Lead. I manage a small remote team, mentor new participants, and contribute to programme design. It's stable, fulfilling work—and I'm proving that disabled people, those with mental health conditions, and neurodivergent individuals can lead, not just participate."

Where They Are Now

  • Role: Sales Team Lead (salaried employee, RemoteAbility)
  • Income: £26,000/year (pro-rata, part-time)
  • Hours: 25 hours/week with full employment rights
  • Next Step: Developing training modules for future cohorts
"RemoteAbility didn't just give me a job—they gave me a career path, respect, and the chance to help others like me. That's priceless."

Success Story Themes: Disability Employment

🕒 "Flexibility Changed Everything"

Self-paced work, adjustable hours, and no commute allowed participants to manage health conditions while building careers.

💡 "I Didn't Know This Was Possible"

Many participants had never considered remote work or digital careers—RemoteAbility opened doors they didn't know existed.

🤝 "The Support Was Different"

Unlike other programmes, RemoteAbility provided ongoing mentoring, wellbeing check-ins, and practical help (not just training).

💰 "Real Earnings, Real Fast"

Participants appreciated earning income during training—not waiting months for 'job-ready' certification before seeing money.

🎯 "Getting the Right Equipment Was a Game-Changer"

Securing equipment, software, and adjustments removed barriers that had previously blocked employment.

🌟 "I Feel Like Myself Again"

The emotional impact of returning to work—identity, purpose, contribution—was as important as the financial gain.
