      What It Is

      A Mobile Event Attraction, Not a Venue

      UNLEASHED travels to events — it doesn't operate from a fixed location. The attraction is built from a modular scaffold/steel structure, with a maximum height of approximately 3 metres, a contained rear, contained sides, a contained roof, and a controlled throwing line. Three throwing lanes face target pedestals inside a staff-only target and reset area.

      Participants stand outside the throwing line and take their shots at approved, suitable smashable targets. The whole set-up is staffed and supervised throughout.

      Why We're Doing It

      UNLEASHED is entertainment with a purpose. Money generated through events helps RemoteAbility create sustainable opportunities, develop social-enterprise projects, and support people towards greater stability, employment and independence.

      What We're Building Next

      RemoteAbility Mobile Food Project

      One of RemoteAbility's initial ambitions is to raise money towards a mobile food project. The plan: UNLEASHED generates income → RemoteAbility creates paid work and supported opportunities → funds help develop the mobile food project → the project can create employment → commercial food sales help make it sustainable → the project can also help provide hot evening meals for people experiencing financial hardship.

      RemoteAbility wants this project to create paid employment opportunities, provide training and experience, generate commercial income, and operate at events and suitable locations — as well as supporting people experiencing financial hardship.
