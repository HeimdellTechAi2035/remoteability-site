Stage Three: Work and Independence

Weeks 5–6 of the Six-Week Pathway to Independence Programme. Real-world action: applying for work, contacting customers, launching services, starting business setup, or continuing towards readiness with structured support.

5️⃣

Week 5: Opportunity, Sales and Customer Skills

🔎 Core Session: Understanding Opportunities

All participants learn how opportunities are found through:

  • Local employers
  • Remote-work platforms
  • Recruitment agencies
  • Community networks
  • LinkedIn
  • Facebook groups
  • Google
  • Local directories
  • Charities
  • Housing associations
  • Business groups
  • Referrals, friends, family and existing contacts

📞 Core Session: Professional Communication

Participants practise:

  • Telephone introductions
  • Emails
  • Messages
  • Follow-ups
  • Meeting preparation
  • Asking questions
  • Listening
  • Explaining value
  • Dealing with rejection
  • Keeping records

💼 Employment Pathway Activity

  • Identify suitable vacancies
  • Adapt their CV
  • Submit supported applications
  • Contact employers
  • Practise interviews
  • Request adjustments
  • Create follow-up messages
  • Record every application

🧑‍💻 Freelance & Business Pathway Activity

  • Build a list of potential customers
  • Research customer problems
  • Create an outreach message
  • Prepare a telephone script
  • Learn basic sales conversations
  • Practise discussing prices
  • Handle objections
  • Begin contacting real prospects

What Sales Actually Means

Participants learn that sales is not:

  • Begging for an opportunity
  • Pressuring people
  • Lying about experience
  • Being the cheapest
  • Sending identical spam to everybody

Good sales means:

  • ✓Understanding a problem
  • ✓Offering a relevant solution
  • ✓Communicating clearly
  • ✓Following up
  • ✓Keeping promises
  • ✓Delivering value

Week 5 Outputs

Employment participants

  • Vacancy list
  • Completed applications
  • Employer contact record
  • Interview scripts
  • Follow-up plan

Freelance & business participants

  • Prospect list
  • Sales script
  • Outreach email
  • Social message
  • Follow-up schedule
  • First customer contacts

Preparation participants

  • Volunteering enquiries
  • Supported-work options
  • Confidence activity
  • Referral progression
  • Next-stage plan
6️⃣

Week 6: AI Tools, Real Action and Progression Plan

Session 1: Building a Personal AI Toolkit

Every participant creates reusable AI prompts suited to their route.

💼 Employment Prompts

  • Improve my CV
  • Tailor my CV to a job
  • Draft a cover letter
  • Explain a job description
  • Practise an interview
  • Draft a follow-up email
  • Identify transferable skills
  • Request reasonable adjustments

🧑‍💻 Freelance & Business Prompts

  • Research a customer
  • Write an introduction email
  • Create a quotation
  • Produce social media content
  • Plan a sales call
  • Write a follow-up message
  • Create a service description
  • Respond to a complaint
  • Plan weekly tasks
  • Review pricing

🌱 Personal-Support Prompts

  • Explain a letter in simple language
  • Prepare questions for an appointment
  • Create a weekly routine
  • Break a task into steps
  • Organise a to-do list
  • Improve a professional message
  • Summarise meeting notes

Session 2: Live Action Session

Participants complete genuine actions with support — different for every participant, but the key requirement is that the action moves them forward. This may include:

  • ✓Applying for jobs
  • ✓Calling an employer
  • ✓Sending business outreach
  • ✓Creating a freelance profile
  • ✓Publishing a business page
  • ✓Registering as self-employed
  • ✓Attending a housing appointment
  • ✓Following up a rehabilitation referral
  • ✓Contacting a volunteering provider
  • ✓Opening a bank account
  • ✓Completing identification applications

Session 3: Final Review

The mentor reviews:

  • Housing situation
  • Recovery and health support
  • Benefits and finances
  • Digital access
  • Confidence
  • Employment readiness
  • Business readiness
  • Applications made
  • Customers contacted
  • Referrals completed
  • Remaining barriers
  • Immediate risks
  • Support required after the programme

Session 4: Ninety-Day Progression Plan

The participant creates a plan covering:

  • What they will do weekly
  • Appointments
  • Applications
  • Customer outreach
  • Training
  • Recovery activity
  • Business setup
  • Income goals
  • Personal wellbeing
  • Mentor support
  • Referral follow-up
  • Review dates

Week 6 Outputs

✓ Personal AI toolkit
✓ Completed real-world actions
✓ Updated progression assessment
✓ Thirty-day action plan
✓ Ninety-day progression plan
✓ Ongoing mentoring schedule
✓ Support contact list
✓ Referral follow-up dates
✓ Employment, freelance or business action tracker
