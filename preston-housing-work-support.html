Housing, recovery and work support in Preston

RemoteAbility CIC, based at 11 Lower Croft in Penwortham, Preston, helps people navigate housing, recovery and work support. The team stays involved with council and homelessness applications, refers people to drug or alcohol treatment where it is needed, and offers a free six-week Pathway to Independence programme towards employment, freelancing or a home-based business. RemoteAbility does not provide emergency accommodation or clinical rehabilitation directly.

If you are in immediate danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999.

If you need to talk to someone right now, Samaritans are free to call, any time, day or night: 116 123.

If you are worried about domestic abuse, the National Domestic Abuse Helpline is free and confidential: 0808 2000 247.

Who it helps

People RemoteAbility supports

Support is open to anyone in these situations. You do not need to fit every group. If you are not sure, request support or contact RemoteAbility.

People sleeping rough

Active help to access emergency accommodation and safety. RemoteAbility does not provide that accommodation itself.

People in temporary accommodation

Support to move towards settled housing and longer-term stability.

People at risk of homelessness

Early support before a housing crisis, including help dealing with the council.

Disabled people

Support built around health, energy and adjustments. The team includes an accessibility lead for reasonable adjustments and assistive tech.

Neurodivergent people

Clear, structured support, with flexible scheduling on the six-week programme.

People recovering from addiction

Referrals, encouragement and coordination alongside recovery. Clinical treatment is provided by other services.

People who cannot manage conventional employment

Flexible routes towards work and independence, at your pace, including preparation and recovery as a valid route.

Veterans

A developing UK-wide network covering housing, wellbeing, benefits, employment and enterprise. It depends on staffing, location and partner availability.

RemoteAbility also continues its youth and community skills initiatives, and its work with employers. Support is fully remote, so it is available anywhere in the United Kingdom, including Preston and Lancashire, and is coordinated with local services.

Services

Housing, recovery and day-to-day stability

This is supported navigation and advocacy. RemoteAbility helps people reach the right service and stays involved. It does not hand over a phone number and walk away. Full detail is on Our Pathway and I Need Support.

Housing and homelessness

  • ✓Understanding urgent needs, including accommodation, food, identification, benefits, and medical or mental-health support
  • ✓Contacting the council homelessness team and completing housing or homelessness applications together
  • ✓Gathering identification and evidence, preparing for appointments, and explaining letters in plain language
  • ✓Connecting people with shelters and supported accommodation, and following up if they are passed between services
  • ✓Help getting a phone, email address or postal address

Recovery

  • ✓Referral to drug or alcohol treatment where needed, with encouragement and follow-up
  • ✓Coordination with housing and recovery organisations, without delivering clinical treatment
  • ✓Domestic-abuse support by connecting people to the right help
  • ✓A safeguarding lead for safety, wellbeing and concerns: safeguarding@remoteability.org.uk

Read the safeguarding policy. Safeguarding concerns are prioritised and responded to within 24 hours.

Stability once the crisis eases

  • ✓Benefits and financial guidance, including Universal Credit, ESA, PIP and Permitted Work
  • ✓Bank account and identification
  • ✓Digital access, a daily routine, confidence and wellbeing
  • ✓Basic skills, communication support, and volunteering or gradual activity at your pace
  • ✓Support maintaining accommodation

The wider pathway is Safe, Stable, Ready, Independent: from crisis, to stability, to skills, to work or self-employment. You can get support at any stage. Nobody is pushed into training.

Programmes

Free six-week Pathway to Independence

The Six-Week Pathway to Independence Programme is the practical course in the Ready and Independent stages. It is free to participants, designed for complete beginners, and delivered remotely anywhere in the UK. If you need equipment or digital access, say so and RemoteAbility will help find a practical solution.

1

Safe and Stable

Weeks 1–2. A full assessment, urgent barriers identified, and referrals or support plans put in place. By the end of week 2, each participant chooses one route.

Ready and Skilled

Work and Independence

Weeks 5–6. The chosen route into employment, freelancing, self-employment or business ownership.

Employment

Part-time, full-time, remote, flexible or supported work, or a gradual return to work.

Freelancing or self-employment

Selling a skill or service independently.

Starting a business

For a viable idea, with support on registration, bookkeeping, branding, websites, pricing and finding customers.

Preparation and recovery

Recovery, volunteering, routine-building and confidence. This is still progress.

How the programme is delivered

  • ✓Two group workshops a week, covering skills, digital tools, employment, business, confidence and practical exercises
  • ✓At least one individual mentoring session each week
  • ✓Practical action sessions for calls, forms, job applications, documents and referral follow-up
  • ✓After six weeks, a ninety-day progression plan and ongoing mentoring, with reviews at completion, thirty days, ninety days, six months and twelve months

When work is the right route, options include accessible employment, remote working, freelancing and a home-based business, with mentoring after launch. The first employer partners named on this website are Heimdell Tech AI (sales and business development) and GreenFix Exterior Care (exterior maintenance). Both are businesses founded by RemoteAbility's founder. More employers are welcome.

Apply for the six-week programme. Applications are reviewed, and RemoteAbility gets in touch within 5 working days.

Common questions

Preston support FAQs

What housing, recovery and work support does RemoteAbility offer in Preston?

RemoteAbility CIC is based at 11 Lower Croft, Penwortham, Preston PR1 9DJ. It helps people contact the council, complete housing and homelessness applications, and connect with shelters and supported accommodation. Where needed, it refers people to drug or alcohol treatment. It also offers a free six-week Pathway to Independence programme towards employment, freelancing or a home-based business. It does not provide emergency accommodation or clinical rehabilitation directly.

Who can get this support?

Support is open to people sleeping rough, people in temporary accommodation, people at risk of homelessness, disabled people, neurodivergent people, people recovering from addiction, and people who struggle with conventional employment. You do not need to fit every category. RemoteAbility is also developing a veterans support network for housing, wellbeing, benefits, employment and enterprise. That network depends on staffing, location and partner availability. Support is fully remote and available anywhere in the UK, including Preston and Lancashire.

Does RemoteAbility provide emergency housing or rehabilitation in Preston?

No. RemoteAbility does not provide emergency accommodation or clinical rehabilitation directly. It helps people access the right services, complete referrals and applications, and stay supported. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. Samaritans are free to call any time on 116 123. The National Domestic Abuse Helpline is free and confidential on 0808 2000 247.

Is the six-week work programme free, and how do I join?

Yes. The Six-Week Pathway to Independence Programme is free, needs no previous experience, and is delivered remotely. Apply on the Apply page. RemoteAbility reviews applications and gets in touch within 5 working days. If you need help with housing or recovery before training, use I Need Support. You can get support at any stage, and you do not have to move into training.
