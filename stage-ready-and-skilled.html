Stage Two: Ready and Skilled

Weeks 3–4 of the Six-Week Pathway to Independence Programme. Building confidence, routines, digital skills and practical work or business skills.

🎯

Main Objective

By the end of Week 4, participants should have improved confidence, basic digital capability, a working routine and the practical skills needed for their selected pathway.

3️⃣

Week 3: Confidence, Routine and Digital Foundations

🗓️ Session 1: Building a Realistic Routine

Participants create a weekly structure covering sleep, appointments, recovery activity, training, meals, exercise, work preparation, family responsibilities, rest, outreach or job applications, and mentor contact.

The routine accounts for disability, neurodivergence, health and energy levels. The aim isn't a conventional nine-to-five — it's consistency.

💬 Session 2: Confidence and Communication

Participants practise:

  • Introducing themselves
  • Explaining their skills
  • Asking for support
  • Speaking to organisations
  • Making telephone calls
  • Attending appointments
  • Handling rejection
  • Setting boundaries
  • Professional behaviour
  • Requesting reasonable adjustments

💻 Session 3: Essential Digital Skills

  • Creating and using email
  • Completing online forms
  • Uploading documents
  • Using video calls
  • Online safety
  • Password security
  • Cloud file storage
  • Calendar use
  • Basic word processing
  • Basic spreadsheets
  • Searching for reliable information
  • Avoiding scams

🤖 Session 4: Introduction to AI

Participants are introduced to accessible AI tools. They learn how AI can help with:

  • Explaining difficult information
  • Drafting emails
  • Planning tasks
  • Preparing for appointments
  • Improving spelling and grammar
  • Writing CV content
  • Developing business ideas
  • Creating customer messages
  • Understanding forms
  • Practising interviews
  • Building daily routines

Participants also learn that AI can be wrong, important information must be checked, personal and confidential information should be protected, AI is a support tool — not a replacement for professional advice — and generated content should always be reviewed and personalised.

Week 3 Outputs

✓ Weekly routine
✓ Communication plan
✓ Professional email account
✓ Digital-skills checklist
✓ Online safety checklist
✓ Personal AI-use guide
✓ List of reasonable adjustments
4️⃣

Week 4: Practical Pathway Skills

Participants now begin work specific to their chosen route from Stage One.

💼

Employment Pathway

  • CV
  • Personal statement
  • Skills profile
  • Job-search plan
  • Reasonable-adjustment statement
  • Interview preparation
  • Job alerts
  • Application tracking
  • Employer research
  • Remote-working readiness
Week 4 outputs: Completed CV, job-search plan, application tracker, interview preparation sheet
🧑‍💻

Freelance Pathway

  • Service definition
  • Target customer
  • Pricing
  • Portfolio or examples
  • Freelancer profile
  • Payment method
  • Customer enquiry process
  • Simple terms
  • Work schedule
  • First prospect list
Week 4 outputs: Defined service, basic price, customer profile, simple freelancer profile, prospect list
🚀

Business Pathway

  • Business idea
  • Customer problem
  • Target market
  • Offer
  • Pricing
  • Business name
  • Business model
  • Startup costs
  • Required registrations
  • Simple business plan
Week 4 outputs: Viable business idea, basic offer, pricing structure, short business plan, startup checklist
🌱

Preparation & Recovery Pathway

  • Confidence
  • Attendance
  • Volunteering opportunities
  • Supported activities
  • Continued health or recovery referrals
  • Digital skills
  • Basic employability
  • Personal stability
  • Next-step planning
Week 4 outputs: Stability plan, referral progress, volunteering or activity plan, updated readiness assessment
